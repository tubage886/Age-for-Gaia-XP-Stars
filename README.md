## Data Access

This stellar catalog is currently being used in an ongoing research project. To protect unpublished research and prevent unauthorized use of the data, access to this repository is currently restricted.

Researchers who wish to obtain and use the catalog may contact **[xkhou@bao.ac.cn](mailto:xkhou@bao.ac.cn)**. Please briefly describe the intended purpose, research topic, and planned use of the data in your request. Data access will be considered on a case-by-case basis.

The complete catalog will be made publicly available after the ongoing research project has been completed.
